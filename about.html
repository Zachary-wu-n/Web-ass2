About Us

Learn about Pet Home's mission, values, and team

Our Mission

Pet Home was established in 2018 with the mission to connect loving families with healthy, quality pet dogs, bringing joy and companionship to every family. We believe pets are not just animals, but family members.

Our Values

❤️ Loving Care

We treat every dog like our own children, ensuring they receive the best care and love.

🏥 Health First

All dogs undergo strict health checks to ensure they join new families in optimal condition.

📚 Professional Guidance

We provide comprehensive feeding guidance and training advice to help new owners better care for their pets.

🤝 Continuous Support

After purchase, we provide 30-day health guarantee and lifetime consultation services to ensure your and your pet's happy life.

Our Process

1
